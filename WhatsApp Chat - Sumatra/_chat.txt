We offer various trekking locations, with prices differing by duration, route difficulty, and how deep we explore into the forest.
 
📍 Location: Ketambe Jungle – Home of Wild Orangutans
 
✅ 2D1N / 3D2N Standard Trek — €32 / person / day
A balanced, relaxed journey focused entirely on wildlife observation. This is authentic rainforest all animals here live completely free and wild. If you are lucky, you will see orangutans, gibbons, macaques, and many bird species; sometimes sightings are frequent and very close, other times they stay hidden deep inside that is the true nature of wild jungle. Even in a short time, the experience is as real and incredible as longer trips.
Team: 1 guide + 1 porter

 ✅ 4 Days / 3 Nights Standard Trek
€ 36 per person / per day.
 
Go deeper into rainforest  plenty of time to observe wild orangutans & wildlife slowly
Balanced pace: comfortable & suitable for all active fitness levels,best value  full immersion without rushing!
 
✅ 5D4N 6D5N Standard Trek — €42 / person / day
More time to move slowly and absorb the deep rainforest atmosphere. We explore wider areas and follow animal trails for longer. With more days inside, your chance of encountering wildlife naturally increases  sometimes we meet groups moving through the trees, other times we follow their tracks for hours without seeing them. Everything depends on where they are that day, we can never predict for sure. The pace is relaxed; this is about immersion, not rushing.
Team: 1 guide + 2 porters
 
✅ 5D4N / 6D5N Jungle Adventure — €65 / person / day
For true explorers only. We take challenging paths, move camp every night, and trek deep into forest rarely visited by people. Here, wildlife lives with very little human contact if you are fortunate, you may see orangutans, sun bears, or clouded leopards; sometimes we find many fresh treks and hear their calls all around us, yet never spot the animal itself. It is wild, unpredictable, and raw. Camps change daily, terrain is steep and difficult, and logistics require a larger, more experienced team  hence the higher cost.
Team: Larger team for safety and support
 
📍 Other Remote Locations (outside Ketambe area)
 
🔹 6D5N / 7D6N Lake Marpunge — €78 / person / day | min. 2 people
Includes transport to the trailhead and return to Ketambe. This route goes deep into dense forest to reach a hidden, beautiful lake. You may walk for days without seeing another person. This is known as elephant territory. If luck is on your side, you will cross paths with wild herds; often we follow their fresh trails, see where they fed or slept, or hear them moving nearby  but seeing them depends entirely on their movement that day. Nothing is guaranteed in the wild. Higher cost due to remote location, difficulty, and risk.
 
🔹 8D7N / 9D8N Kappi Location — €93 / person / day | min. 2 people
Extremely dense, ancient forest with a very unique, quiet atmosphere unlike anywhere else. This is home to wild elephants, Sumatran tigers (rare but present), sun bears, and many primates. If you are lucky, you will see them; more often, you will find clear signs: footprints, scratch marks, or places where they passed just hours before. You are entering their home — we are only guests here, and sightings are never certain.
 
✨ What makes Kappi special:
 
1. Sulfur / Toxic Water Spring
Bluish, cloudy water with a strong sulfur smell, rich in minerals and toxic substances. During dry seasons, thirsty animals (deer, pigs, monkeys, birds) unknowingly drink here and often perish nearby  you will see bones and remains around it. A powerful reminder of how wild this place is.
2. Pristine Nature
Deep inside Gunung Leuser National Park: giant trees, crystal rivers, hidden waterfalls, and pure fresh air. Safe, clear springs wind through the forest everywhere.
3. Wildlife Habitat
Siamang gibbons, leaf monkeys, sun bears, Sumatran tigers, deer, wild boar, and rare hornbills live here freely. We may hear their calls all around us, follow their trails for hours, or sometimes see them clearly  but there is never a guarantee. That is the mystery and beauty of wild jungle.
 
This place is unique: beautiful, mysterious, where life and danger exist side by side. Perfect if you want real, untamed adventure.
Requires a larger team of guides and porters for safety and logistics
